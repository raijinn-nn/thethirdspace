The Sociological Concept

What is "The Third Space"?

In 1989, urban sociologist Ray Oldenburg coined the term "the third place" in his seminal book, The Great Good Place.

Oldenburg observed that human happiness and mental well-being rely on three primary realms:

  • 1st Space: The Home — where you rest, unwind, and live privately.
  • 2nd Space: The Workplace / School — where you perform, deliver, and face deadlines.
  • 3rd Space: The Neutral Public Ground — cozy cafes, libraries, and gathering spots where you belong unconditionally.

We created the third space to honor this principle: a community sanctuary free from haste, where ideas flow freely and espresso is served with mindful artistry.

"The third place hosts the regular, voluntary, informal, and happily anticipated gatherings of individuals beyond home and work."

— Ray Oldenburg (1989)
