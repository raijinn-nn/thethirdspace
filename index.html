The Concept

"The Third Place" — where community meets serenity.

Sociologist Ray Oldenburg defined the "third place" as the social surroundings separate from the two usual environments of home (the first place) and the workplace or school (the second place).

At the third space, we created an environment that doesn't rush you. Whether you're debugging code for hours, studying for board exams, or meeting a friend for pour-over coffee, our space is yours.
